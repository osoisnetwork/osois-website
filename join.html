Before You

Still Have Questions? →
Do I need experience to apply?+

No — many OSOIS members start with little to no experience and are trained through the OSOIS Academy before joining the active network.

Is there a fee to apply or join?+

Applying is free. Academy training has associated costs, which are explained during the review and onboarding process.

What happens after I apply?+

Applications are reviewed by the OSOIS team. Selected applicants receive instructions for the next Academy step, which may include an open call or interview.

I'm a brand or client. Where do I start?+

Use the Contact page to tell us about your project. The Academy application is for models and creatives entering the OSOIS development pathway.

What happens after I'm accepted?+

Accepted applicants enter the Academy pathway. Graduation and meeting the OSOIS standard are required before entry into the professional Network.

Is OSOIS only for one category of talent?+

Not at all — OSOIS is a network for models, photographers, videographers, HMUA, stylists, creatives, and production crew alike.
