How do I join OSOIS?+

Models and creatives begin with the OSOIS Academy application. Clients and partners can use this contact form to start a conversation.

Do you work with brands and businesses?+

Yes — OSOIS Production partners with brands and businesses on photo, video, and full campaign work. Reach out to get a quote.

Do I need to go through the Academy first?+

Yes. Models and creatives complete the Academy pathway and meet the OSOIS standard before joining the professional Network.

Where are you located?+

We're based in Fort Wayne, Indiana. In-person meetings are available by appointment only.

How do I book a production?+

Select "Book a Production" as your subject in the contact form above, and our team will follow up with a custom quote.

Can I collaborate or partner with OSOIS?+

Absolutely — we're always open to meaningful collaborations and partnerships. Tell us about it in the message field above.
